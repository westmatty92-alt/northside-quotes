There are no other new findings. I checked these parts of the fix and found them sound:
- **Lock order:** the nonce lock is always taken before the address lock, and nothing takes them the other way round, so they cannot deadlock.
- **Lock scope:** advisory locks are transaction-scoped, and each RPC runs as one transaction.
- **Waiting calls:** under READ COMMITTED, each statement after the lock gets a fresh snapshot, so a waiting call sees the winner's row.
- **Job claim:** `claim_ghl_job` uses `for update skip locked` on a `limit 1` subquery.
- **Grants:** the service-role grants match every write in the invoker functions.
- **Storage reads:** the storage select policy evaluates `quote_photos`' own RLS as the caller.
- **Nothing removed or loosened:** no client write policy was added, and no `force`, revoke, check or rate limit was removed.
