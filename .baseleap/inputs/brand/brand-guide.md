# Northside Plumbing — brand guide (TEST client, invented for the rehearsal)

**Personality:** a family trade business in Hamilton's North End. Dependable, quick, plain-spoken, a bit of steel-town grit.
Not corporate, not cute. "We show up, we fix it, we tell you the price first."

## Colours
| Name | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Harbour Teal | #0B4F6C | Primary: header, primary buttons, links |
| Copper | #C8742B | Accent: highlights, the pipe mark, emergency emphasis (large text/graphics only on white) |
| Steel Ink | #14212B | Body text |
| Paper | #FAF7F2 | Page background |
| Fog | #E6E1D8 | Cards, dividers |

## Type
- Headings: **Barlow Semi Condensed**, SemiBold/Bold (Google Font): industrial, trade-sign feel.
- Body: **Source Sans 3**, Regular/SemiBold (Google Font).

## Logo
`northside-wordmark.svg`: a copper pipe elbow beside the wordmark "NORTHSIDE" with "PLUMBING · HAMILTON" underneath.
Minimum height 28 px on screens. On teal, the wordmark text is Paper.

## Voice
Short sentences. Say what happens next and when. Prices and times are concrete ("We'll call you within one business day").
