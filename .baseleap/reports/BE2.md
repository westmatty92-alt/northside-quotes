### BUG-QA2-3 (sign-in takes `text/plain` as JSON)
- **Root cause:** `src/app/api/auth/sign-in/route.ts` called `readJsonBody(req)`, which does `JSON.parse(await req.text())` whatever the `Content-Type`. A cross-site `text/plain` post (a "simple request", no preflight) was parsed, counted as a failed attempt and answered 401.
- **Reproducing test:** `tests/api/auth/sign-in-content-type.test.ts` (staging counters, throwaway `@be2-stub.test` emails, Auth's answer for them given locally, so no real account and no Auth rate limit is touched). Before the fix: **9 failed, 3 passed** (each refused type got 401 instead of 400). After: **12 passed**.
- **Fix:** `requireJsonContentType(req)` in `src/lib/server/http.ts` (media type before any `;`, trimmed, lower-cased, must equal `application/json`; otherwise `ProblemError(400, 'bad_request')`), called first in the sign-in handler, before the body is read and before anything is counted.
- **What the test pins:** `text/plain` (QA's case), `text/plain;charset=UTF-8`, no Content-Type, empty Content-Type, `application/x-www-form-urlencoded`, `multipart/form-data`, `application/jsonp`, `application/json-patch+json`, and `text/plain; x=application/json` all give 400 `bad_request`, no cookie, **zero** Supabase Auth calls, **zero** `sign_in_attempts` rows for the address and the email. `application/json`, `application/json; charset=utf-8` and `APPLICATION/JSON` still reach Auth and give the usual 401 with one counted attempt.
- **Other JSON-body routes:** the only JSON-body route under BE2's paths is sign-in. The same helper is not applied to the BE1/BE3 routes that call `readJsonBody` (`POST /api/quote-requests`, `status-changes`, `quote`): they are outside this step's paths, and they are not open to the cross-site case (`POST /api/quote-requests` needs the custom `X-Form-Token` header, which forces a preflight; the two staff routes need `Authorization: Bearer`, which does too).
