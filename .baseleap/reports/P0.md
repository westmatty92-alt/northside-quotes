## Open questions for Matthew (relay; reply like "1A, 2A, ...")
The PRD already uses the recommended option (A) everywhere; a different answer changes the PRD before Gate 1 approval.
1. Spam protection with no puzzle. A honeypot + 3-second minimum + 5 submissions per 10 minutes per network address (recommended) / B add Cloudflare Turnstile (outside account, opens a spend gate) / C simple maths question.
2. Photo rules. A optional, up to 3, 5 MB each, JPEG, PNG or WebP (recommended) / B at least 1 required / C also accept HEIC from iPhones.
3. Urgency options. A three: Emergency (active leak or no water, today), Soon (within a few days), Flexible (within two weeks) (recommended) / B two: Emergency, Not urgent / C four, adding "This week".
4. Reference number. A `NQ-` plus 6 random characters, not sequential, for example `NQ-7K3M9P` (recommended) / B sequential `NQ-0001` / C date-based.
5. Weekly count. A Monday to Sunday Hamilton time; Won and Lost counted by the date of the status change (recommended) / B Won counted by request date / C rolling last 7 days.
6. How the two accounts are created. A created by Baseleap before staging, no in-app screen, no password-reset email (recommended) / B invite emails (needs an email sender, conflicts with $0 and no email).
7. Notifications in v1. A none at all (recommended) / B an on-screen "new since you last looked" badge only / C emails to a test inbox (needs a provider).
8. GHL copy when GHL is down. A save first, automatic retry at least every 24 hours, up to 5 retries, then shown as failed (recommended) / B retry once then manual button only / C no retry, log only. The architect confirms whether the free hosting plan can retry faster than daily.
9. Primary metric target. A 24 hours or less median to first quote, confirmed after a 14-day baseline (recommended) / B 4 business hours / C none until the baseline exists.
10. Real client only: retention and consent wording. A keep 24 months then purge by hand, consent line as in US-001 (recommended) / B keep until deletion is requested / C 12 months.
11. For the architect: can the reference number and problem text be attached to the GHL contact without extra GHL setup? A yes, include them / B contact fields only (used until answered).
12. Real client only: is GHL's own hosting location acceptable for Canadian personal data? A confirm before any real client goes live (recommended) / B accept without checking.

Defaults I settled from the brief without needing Matthew (still shown in the PRD): photos are optional; v1 sends nothing; the price is a note in CAD with no tax; any status can move to any other; the inbox pages 50 at a time; both accounts have identical rights.
