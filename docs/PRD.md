# PRD: Northside quote requests

## Overview

### Problem
When a homeowner in Hamilton has a plumbing problem, the three-person Northside shop struggles to catch the quote request, because requests arrive by phone, text and Facebook and some get lost. That costs Northside jobs it never knew it was offered. Today they scroll three channels and rely on memory (brief, "The problem").

### Job to be done
When a homeowner contacts me about a plumbing problem, I want every request to land in one place with the details and photos, so I can reply, record my price, and know which jobs I won.

### Personas
| Persona | Who they are | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Homeowner | A Hamilton homeowner with a plumbing problem who wants a quote, usually on a phone, with no account | Brief, "What they asked for" (mobile-first public page, up to 3 photos, no customer accounts); intake answer 1 |
| Owner | Runs Northside; wants one inbox, to add a price and note, and to mark won or lost | Brief, "The problem"; intake answer 1 |
| Office helper | Works alongside the owner with identical rights (same inbox, same actions) | Brief, "Constraints" (two users, same rights); intake answer 7 |

The research record for this client is empty (no website, no current stack, no research brief). No customer quotes, volumes or baselines are claimed anywhere in this PRD; where a number is a design assumption it says so.

### Why now
This is the full-team rehearsal: it exercises every phase on throwaway accounts with a fake client. For the (fake) client, the cost of waiting is more lost requests; the real purpose is a small, complete product to run the pipeline end to end.

### Severity note
Frequency is unknown (no research). If this were a real client, the first step would be to count requests per week for 14 days. Pain per lost request is high (a lost job), so the build is justified on severity, not frequency.

## Goals & Success Metrics
- **Primary metric:** Median hours to first quote — baseline unknown (measure for 14 days first) → target 24 hours or less within 30 days after launch, decided as OQ-9A and confirmed after the 14-day baseline (full definition in `docs/metrics-spec.md`)
- **Guardrails:** GHL copy success rate (of requests whose homeowner ticked the offers box) must stay at or above 95% within 24 hours; Photo upload failure rate must stay at or below 5 per 100 submitted requests; Blocked submission share must stay at or below 20% of attempts (all thresholds proposed, set in `docs/metrics-spec.md`)
- **Rehearsal definition of done (intake answer 1):** the Playwright E2E on the staging preview passes: homeowner submits with photos → sees a reference number → owner signs in and sees it at the top of the inbox (the Requests tab, where sign-in lands) → adds price and note → marks it won (by dragging its card on the Pipeline tab or from the Requests tab) → the weekly count updates. After Gate 3, the production health URL returns 200.

## Scope

### In scope (v1)
Spine: a homeowner submits a request with photos and gets a reference number; the owner signs in, sees it at the top of the inbox (the Requests tab), adds a price and note, marks it won (by dragging its card on the Pipeline tab or from the Requests tab), and the weekly count updates.

Pipelines live in our app on every hybrid build. The signed-in area has two tabs, each with its own address that can be bookmarked: **Requests** (the list, newest first, with filter and search) and **Pipeline** (the board with columns New, Quoted, Won and Lost, showing every request). They are separate tabs, not a toggle (navigation rule of 2026-11-20); signing in lands on Requests. GoHighLevel's role is website hosting and email marketing only; it receives a contact and a stage tag only for homeowners who tick the optional, unticked-by-default "offers" box on the public form (ruling 1B, 2026-11-20), one contact per homeowner even if she sends several requests, and it is never the source of truth. Requests without the tick never go to GoHighLevel.

- Public Request a quote page with validation, reference number and the optional offers box (unticked, stored with its time) — US-001
- Up to 3 private photos per request — US-002
- Spam protection without a puzzle, with a capped blocked-submission event log — US-003
- Sign-in for the two users, invite-only, passwords set by Baseleap, per-email and per-address lockout, tabs that land on Requests — US-004
- Requests tab (the inbox list) and request detail, newest first, with photos — US-005
- Price and note per request — US-006

MoSCoW summary: 9 Must, 5 Should, 3 Could, 17 stories in total; Musts are 53% of stories (9 of 17). The 2026-11-20 rulings add no story: the offers box and its stored consent are criteria inside US-001 (a Must, because consent is data protection and is never deferred), the lockout and password rules are inside US-004, the tabs are inside US-004, US-005 and US-016, and the event cap is inside US-003. Won't-this-time items are the non-goals below. Cut order if the run overruns: US-013, US-014, US-015, US-012, US-011, US-017, US-010, US-009 (the stage-tag push goes before the copy it depends on).

- **NG-2** No CAPTCHA, Turnstile or any third-party bot challenge — needs an outside account, and the $0 limit applies — revisit only if the honeypot, time trap and rate limit let spam through (see OQ-1).
- **NG-3** No customer accounts and no page where a homeowner looks up a request's status; the reference number is the only receipt — brief says no customer accounts — revisit after the owner asks for it.
- **NG-4** No in-app user management, public sign-up, in-app password change, "forgot password" link, password-reset email, magic links or social sign-in; Baseleap creates the two accounts, sets each password, hands it over by phone, and resets a forgotten one from the Supabase dashboard (ruling 3A, 2026-11-20) — email-based sign-in flows need an email provider and the $0 limit allows none — revisit with NG-1.
- **NG-5** No payments, deposits, invoices, tax calculation or Stripe; the price is a note of what was quoted — brief and intake exclude Stripe — revisit as a separate project.
- **NG-6** No two-way GoHighLevel sync, no GHL pipelines, opportunities or workflows built by us (the pipeline lives in our app), no photo copy, and no contact with any real GHL account — GHL is for website hosting and email marketing only, and in v1 it receives the one-way contact copy and stage tags only, and only for homeowners who ticked the offers box — revisit after the copy has run in production for a month.
- **NG-7** No deleting, archiving, exporting or editing of submitted requests in the app; a privacy deletion request is handled by Baseleap by hand — keeps v1 small — revisit with the retention decision (OQ-10).
- **NG-8** No scheduling, calendar, job management, service-area check, maps or address lookup; the address is free text — outside the quote-request job — revisit after 30 days of use.
- **NG-9** No roles beyond the two equal users, and no assigning requests to a person — same rights by decision — revisit if the shop grows.
- **NG-10** No reporting beyond the current week's three numbers: no past weeks, charts, trends or CSV export — the brief asks for a weekly count only — revisit after a month of data.
- **NG-11** No marketing website, no embedding on a GHL page, and no analytics tools such as GA4 or Meta Pixel; events go to our own table only — GHL's website-hosting role is not used in v1 because no website was asked for — revisit if a marketing site is commissioned.
- **NG-12** No languages other than English, no quote PDFs, no reply templates — keeps v1 small — revisit on request.
- **NG-13** No AI features of any kind — not asked for — not planned.
- **NG-14** No editing or adding of pipeline columns: the board has exactly New, Quoted, Won and Lost, and no one can add, rename, remove, recolour or reorder a column — the four statuses are fixed by FR-12 and Matthew's ruling of 2026-11-19 — revisit if the owner asks for another stage.
- **NG-15** No manual ordering of cards inside a column, no per-column totals, swimlanes, saved board layouts or card colours; cards stay newest first by submitted time — keeps the board small — revisit on request.
- **NG-16** No consent management in the app: no page, link or button where a homeowner changes or withdraws her offers choice, and no editing of a stored consent; a person who no longer wants offers tells Northside, who removes the contact in GoHighLevel by hand — keeps v1 small, and the test account holds no real contacts — revisit before any real client goes live (OQ-16).

## Functional Requirements
- **FR-1** The public Request page collects name (2–100 characters), phone (10 digits after removing spaces, dashes, brackets and an optional leading +1), email (valid format, up to 254 characters), address (5–200 characters), problem (10–2,000 characters) and urgency (exactly one of Emergency, Soon, Flexible, none preselected). All six are required, each invalid or empty field shows its own message next to it, and entered values are kept on any refusal. The form also has one optional checkbox labelled "Email me offers and news from Northside. This is optional." that is never ticked when the page loads, is not required, and keeps the state the homeowner gave it on any refusal (ruling 1B). The page shows the line "Northside will use these details to reply to your quote request. It will email you offers only if you tick the box." Serves: US-001
- **FR-2** Every stored request gets a unique reference number in the form `NQ-` plus 6 characters from A–Z and 2–9 excluding I, L and O (for example `NQ-7K3M9P`), that is not sequential. It is shown on the confirmation and in the inbox, and is never issued for a request that was not stored. Serves: US-001
- **FR-3** A request and all of its selected photos are stored together or not at all: the confirmation and reference number appear only after everything is stored; on any failure the homeowner sees the failure message, keeps everything she entered, and nothing partial is visible in the inbox; a double submit creates one request. Serves: US-001, US-002
- **FR-4** Submitting a request sends no email or SMS and calls no email or SMS service. Serves: US-001
- **FR-5** The Request page is usable within 3 seconds on a simulated 4G phone and passes an automated WCAG 2.1 AA check with 0 serious or critical violations. Serves: US-001
- **FR-6** A request takes 0 to 3 photos; each photo is JPEG, PNG or WebP (checked by content), at most 5 MB (5,242,880 bytes); a refused or failed photo shows its message next to that photo while the rest of the form is kept. Serves: US-002
- **FR-7** Photos are private: only the two signed-in users can see them, and a direct address for a photo returns nothing to anyone who is not signed in. A photo that cannot be loaded shows "Photo unavailable". Serves: US-002, US-005, US-012
- **FR-8** The public form is protected by a hidden honeypot field, a minimum 3 seconds between page load and submit, and a limit of 5 accepted submissions per 10 minutes per network address; refusals store nothing, issue no reference number, and do not reveal which check failed; if the rate-limit counter cannot be read the request is accepted; no third-party challenge is loaded. The refusal log is capped so spam cannot grow it without bound (SEC-14): at most one `spam_submission_blocked` event per network address and reason in any 10-minute window, whose `count` property holds every refusal of that address and reason in the window, and at most 1,000 such events per day in total (Hamilton day); refusals past a cap are still refused exactly the same way and are simply not logged as new events (the daily ceiling means the blocked-share guardrail reads low on a flood day, which is accepted). Serves: US-003
- **FR-9** Exactly two accounts (owner, office helper) with identical rights sign in with email and password; the inbox (both tabs) and all request and photo data require sign-in; there is no public sign-up; a session lasts at most 7 days. Sign-in is limited two ways (ruling 4A, SEC-11): (1) 5 failed attempts for one email within 15 minutes pause sign-in for that email for 5 minutes, and the count restarts when the pause ends; (2) 20 failed attempts from one network address within 15 minutes, across any emails, pause sign-in from that address for 15 minutes, and its count also restarts when the pause ends. A paused sign-in is refused even with the right password, with the message "Too many sign-in attempts. Try again in a few minutes." that does not say which limit applied or whether the email has an account; an email with no account is counted and paused exactly like one with an account; a successful sign-in is not counted; if the attempt counters cannot be read, sign-in is refused with "Sign-in is unavailable. Try again in a few minutes." Trade-off, accepted by this ruling: anyone who knows the owner's email can keep her signed out in 5-minute stretches by failing 5 times again each time (it was 15 minutes at a time before), and people who share a network address with a guesser, such as an office or a phone carrier, are paused together for 15 minutes; she waits, or phones Baseleap. Guessing is capped at 1,440 attempts a day per email and 1,920 a day per address. Serves: US-004
- **FR-10** The Requests tab (the inbox list) lists requests newest first by submitted time, 50 at a time with a "Show more" control, each row showing reference, name, urgency, status and submitted time (Hamilton time); opening a request shows every submitted field, the phone as a tap-to-call link, the email as an email link, and the photos; it works at 375 px width without horizontal scrolling and shows the first 50 rows within 2 seconds. Serves: US-005
- **FR-11** The owner or helper can save one price (CAD, 0.01 to 99,999.99, at most 2 decimals, stored to the cent as typed) and one note (up to 1,000 characters) per request; either may be blank; a new price replaces the old one; saving over someone else's newer change is refused with a reload message and overwrites nothing. Serves: US-006
- **FR-12** The owner or helper can set a request's status to New, Quoted, Won or Lost, from any status to any other, from the Requests tab or by moving its card on the Pipeline tab (FR-23); each real change stores the previous status, the new status, the time and the user; selecting the current status (or dropping a card back in its own column) changes nothing; status and price changes never reorder the list. Serves: US-007, US-016
- **FR-13** The Requests tab and the Pipeline tab each show three numbers for the current week (Monday 00:00:00 to Sunday 23:59:59 America/Toronto): Requests this week (by submitted time), Won (requests currently Won whose last change to Won was this week) and Lost (same rule for Lost); the numbers update within 2 seconds of a status save; if the numbers cannot be loaded the list still loads. Serves: US-008
- **FR-14** The GoHighLevel copy never blocks, delays beyond 3 seconds, or loses a request: the request is stored and confirmed first, and any copy failure (GHL down, slow, token missing or rejected) leaves the request and its confirmation untouched. Stage-tag pushes follow the same rule (FR-24). Serves: US-001, US-009
- **FR-15** After a request is stored whose homeowner ticked the offers box (FR-25), that homeowner's contact, with the request's name, phone, email, address and the tag `quote-request`, exists in the GoHighLevel test sub-account within 2 minutes. There is one contact per homeowner (ruling 2A): before creating a contact the app looks for an existing one with the same email or phone, using the match rule in `docs/integration-contract.md`; if one exists, the request is linked to it and no second contact is created, so a second request from the same person updates the same contact. The only change the app ever makes to an existing contact is adding or removing its own tags (`quote-request` and the stage tags, FR-24), never its name, phone, email or address, so the details on a later request are not written over the first. A request whose homeowner did not tick the box is never sent to GoHighLevel: no contact is created or changed, no tag is pushed and no GHL call is made for it. The copy is one-way, never creates a second contact for the same homeowner or a duplicate for a retried attempt, and never includes photos; each request shows its copy state (Copied, Waiting to retry, Failed, or "Not copied: no offers consent"); a waiting request is retried at least every 24 hours until it succeeds or has had 5 retries after the first attempt; copy records hold no token and no personal data. Serves: US-009, US-010
- **FR-16** The Requests tab can be filtered to All, New, Quoted, Won or Lost, keeping newest-first order; the Pipeline tab is not filtered and shows every request (its columns already are the statuses). Serves: US-011
- **FR-17** A photo opens at full size from its thumbnail and keeps opening for as long as the user is signed in. Serves: US-012
- **FR-18** On the Requests tab the owner can search by reference number, name or phone, case-insensitively, with partial name match. Serves: US-013
- **FR-19** A request with a failed copy has a Retry control that creates at most one contact; a request that was not copied for lack of offers consent has no Retry control, and a retry asked for directly is refused and creates nothing. Serves: US-014
- **FR-20** A request shows who last changed it and when, or "Not updated yet". Serves: US-015
- **FR-21** The server records the events listed in `docs/metrics-spec.md` when the stated trigger happens, with ids only and no personal data in event properties. Serves: US-001, US-002, US-003, US-004, US-005, US-006, US-007, US-008, US-009, US-010, US-011, US-012, US-013, US-014, US-015, US-016, US-017
- **FR-22** All personal data and photos are stored in Canada (Supabase ca-central-1), and the GoHighLevel copy and stage tags go only to the test sub-account and only for requests whose homeowner ticked the offers box. Serves: US-001, US-002, US-004, US-009, US-017
- **FR-23** The Pipeline tab (the board) is its own tab with its own address (FR-26), separate from the Requests tab (the newest-first list of FR-10, which stays as built); there is no List / Board switch. The Pipeline tab shows four fixed columns in the order New, Quoted, Won, Lost; each column lists its requests newest first by submitted time, 50 at a time with a "Show more" control, and an empty column shows "No new requests", "No quoted requests", "No won requests" or "No lost requests". Each card shows reference number, name, urgency and submitted time (Hamilton time), and opens the request detail. The owner or helper changes a request's status by dragging its card to another column, or by the card's "Move to" menu, which lists the other three statuses and works with keyboard, screen reader and a single tap with no dragging; Escape or a drop outside the columns cancels a drag. Both ways follow FR-12 and have the same effects (stored change, weekly count, events, stage tag). The card moves at once and shows "Saving"; if the save fails, the card returns to its original column and position, "Couldn't save. Try again." shows and nothing is stored; if someone else changed the request first, the card returns and "This request was changed by someone else. Reload to see the latest." shows; if the session has expired, the card returns, nothing is saved and the user is sent to sign-in. Dropping a card in the Quoted column changes the status only; it does not set a price and does not count as the first quote. The weekly count (FR-13) shows on both tabs. The Pipeline tab shows its first 50 cards per column within 2 seconds, needs no horizontal page scrolling at 375 px width, and passes an automated WCAG 2.1 AA check with 0 serious or critical violations. Serves: US-016
- **FR-24** Every real status change, from the Requests tab or the Pipeline tab, of a request whose homeowner ticked the offers box updates that request's GoHighLevel contact tags; a status change on a request whose homeowner did not tick the box makes no GHL call and changes nothing on any contact, even when the same person has another request that was copied. The contact always carries `quote-request` and exactly one stage tag from `quote-new`, `quote-quoted`, `quote-won`, `quote-lost` that matches the status of the request that was copied or changed last; the previous stage tag is removed in the same update. When one contact is shared by several requests from the same person (FR-15), the most recent copy or status change among them decides the stage tag (last change wins), as in `docs/integration-contract.md`. A contact created or linked by the copy (FR-15) gets `quote-request` plus the stage tag for the request's status at that moment. Tags the app did not add are never removed and no other contact field is changed. The push happens after the status change is stored and never blocks, delays beyond 3 seconds, reverses or loses the status change. If it fails because GHL is down or slow, the request detail shows "Stage tag waiting to retry", it is retried at least every 24 hours up to 5 retries after the first attempt (the rule of OQ-8A), then shows "Stage tag failed"; a missing or rejected token shows "Stage tag failed" at once, as for the copy (AC-009.3); every retry applies the request's current status, not an older one, and a later status change starts a new push with its own retries. If the contact does not exist yet, the push waits for the contact copy; if the copy ends as failed, the stage tag shows "Stage tag failed". When the push succeeds the request detail shows "Stage tag up to date". Failure records hold no token and no personal data. Serves: US-017
- **FR-25** Each stored request records whether the homeowner ticked the offers box (yes or no) and, when yes, the time the request was stored as the time of consent; a submission that arrives without the offers value, or with any value other than a clear yes, is stored as no. The choice cannot be changed afterwards in the app (NG-16). A request is stored and confirmed in the same way whether or not the box was ticked; the choice only decides whether anything is sent to GoHighLevel (FR-15, FR-24). Serves: US-001, US-009, US-010, US-017
- **FR-26** The signed-in area has two tabs in its main navigation, Requests and Pipeline. Each tab has its own address that can be bookmarked and opened directly, and the current tab is marked as current; nothing in the app is a toggle between them. Signing in lands on Requests. A signed-out visitor who opens either tab's address is sent to sign-in and, after signing in, lands on the tab whose address she opened. Serves: US-004, US-005, US-016
- **FR-27** Baseleap sets each of the two passwords and hands it to its owner by phone, and resets a forgotten one from the Supabase dashboard (ruling 3A); the app has no screen or link to change or reset a password and sends no reset email. Each password Baseleap sets is at least 12 characters long and the two accounts never share a password (OQ-17, option A, ledger ruling 2026-11-20); this is a rule for Baseleap when it sets or resets a password, since the app has no screen where a password is typed in for the first time. Serves: US-004

## Technical Considerations
- **System of record:** our app (Supabase, ca-central-1) owns every quote request, photo, price, note and status. GoHighLevel never owns or changes a request.
- **Platform roles:** our app = public form, owner inbox with a Requests tab (list) and a Pipeline tab (board), all pipelines (standard on every hybrid build) and source of truth; GoHighLevel test sub-account = website hosting and email marketing only (v1 builds no website), fed by the one-way contact copy tagged `quote-request` plus one stage tag, one contact per homeowner, only for homeowners who ticked the offers box; non-authoritative, never blocks or loses a request; Vercel = hosting for the app and the production health URL; Supabase ca-central-1 = database, sign-in and private photo storage. No Stripe, no n8n, Zapier or Make, no email or SMS provider, no CAPTCHA vendor.
- **Data:** nothing exists to migrate (research record is empty). New data: quote requests, photos, price, note, status changes, copy state, events. Volume is a design assumption, not evidence: up to 200 requests a month, up to 600 photos a month, 2 users. Personal data collected from homeowners: name, phone, email, home address, and photos that may show the inside of a home, plus a yes or no "offers" consent and the time it was given (decided, ruling 1B). Retention for a real client is undecided (OQ-10); in the rehearsal all data is test data.
- **Roles & permissions:** anonymous visitors can only submit a request (and see their own confirmation); the owner and the office helper have identical rights to read and change everything; no other roles; no customer accounts. Row-level security applies to every table and the photo bucket is private (house stack, intake answer 4).
- **Integrations:** GoHighLevel via one private-integration token for the test sub-account (variable name set by devops and the integration engineer; Matthew sets the value; no value appears in any file or log). No other third party. Technical flags for the architect: the free hosting plan may limit how often a background retry can run (US-010 asks for at least every 24 hours); the GHL copy needs a way to avoid duplicate contacts when a timed-out attempt is retried (AC-009.4) and to link a second request from the same person (same email or same phone, the match rule already in `docs/integration-contract.md`) to the one existing contact (AC-009.10, AC-009.11); the copy and the tag push happen only for requests with consent "yes" (FR-25), so the request needs a stored consent value and its time before any GHL job is queued, and a missing or unclear value counts as "no"; whether the reference number and problem text can be attached to the GHL contact without extra GHL setup is open (OQ-11). Stage tags (FR-24) need the architect to confirm that the test sub-account lets the integration add and remove tags on a contact, and whether a tag that does not exist yet is created automatically (OQ-15). The Pipeline tab (FR-23) needs a drag interaction that also works by tap and keyboard without dragging; the designer and architect decide how, the PRD fixes only the behaviour. Navigation (FR-26) needs two separate addresses, one per tab, that each work when opened directly; the architect chooses the route names. Sign-in limits (FR-9) need a per-email counter and a per-network-address counter that survive across server instances; where they live and how long the address is kept is the architect's call and goes in `docs/SECURITY_MODEL.md`. Passwords (FR-27) are set and reset outside the app, from the Supabase dashboard.
- **Money:** no payment is taken, but price is a money value: Canadian dollars, stored to the cent with no rounding, no tax added.
- **Personal data and residency:** all data and photos stay in ca-central-1. The GHL copy puts name, phone, email and address in GoHighLevel, and only for homeowners who ticked the unticked-by-default offers box (ruling 1B, which resolves security finding SEC-6: nothing is enrolled in email marketing without a tick, and the form's data-use line says so). Nothing verifies the email or phone, so a tick is the homeowner's word, not proof of ownership of the address; whether that is enough for a real client under Canadian anti-spam and privacy law is OQ-16. GHL's own hosting location is not verified and is only acceptable here because the sub-account is a test account (OQ-12 for a real client).
- **Constraints:** mobile-first public page; Request page usable in 3 seconds on a simulated 4G phone; WCAG 2.1 AA check on the public page and on the Pipeline tab; Requests tab first 50 rows (and first 50 cards per column) in 2 seconds; all times shown in America/Toronto; English only; $0 spend (anything paid opens a spend approval).
